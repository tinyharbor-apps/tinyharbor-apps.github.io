English

GreenClock is a desktop app that works on your computer. We do not run any server for it, we have no user accounts, and we do not collect analytics, tracking or advertising data.

What stays on your computer

  • Your crops, notes, photos, tasks and backups are saved only in GreenClock's data folder on your computer. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • To recognise your computer for the licence, GreenClock creates an anonymous code derived from it. It is stored only in the local licence file and is never sent.

When GreenClock connects to the internet

  • Licence (Gumroad). When you activate GreenClock, and then about once a week while you are online, the app sends your licence key to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to check that it is valid. Gumroad replies with the details of your purchase; GreenClock keeps your purchase email in the local licence file, only to show you who the licence belongs to.
  • New versions (GitHub Pages). At most once a day, while you are online, the app reads a small public file from tinyharbor-apps.github.io that contains the number of the latest version. Nothing about you or your notes is sent. Like any website, GitHub may record technical data of the request (such as the IP address) under its own privacy policy.
  • Links. Links you click (for example to Gumroad) open in your normal web browser.

Without internet, GreenClock keeps working; only activation needs a connection.

Purchase and emails

The purchase, payment and licence key are handled by Gumroad, under Gumroad's privacy policy. As the seller we receive from Gumroad the usual order details (such as your email and the order). We use them only to deliver the product, handle refunds and answer you. If you subscribe to our updates on Gumroad, we send only news about our apps; you can unsubscribe at any time.

We never sell or share your data.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: tinyharbor.apps@gmail.com

Deutsch

Was auf deinem Computer bleibt

Gumroad antwortet mit den Kaufdaten; GreenClock speichert die E-Mail-Adresse des Kaufs in der lokalen Lizenzdatei, nur um dir zu zeigen, auf wen die Lizenz läuft.
